## Implementation
Removed premature non-social habit increase from Life._assign. A generic solo action now records a persisted commitment only after arrival at an accessible, uniquely reserved, physically reached station (within 0.12m). The commitment is consumed on completion, checking intent, stable station, room, time and physical position; releasing a station cancels it. Sleep credit additionally requires own home. Catalog browsing requires a concrete extant item; art viewing requires the actual object and its matching interaction station plus successful object-view event. An expired solo activity recruited into a group is settled before release. Disabling an actor at its due wake cancels the marker. Legacy perform states without a marker receive no fabricated completion credit.
Reading credit follows a successfully settled full reading interval, not an interrupted partial session (partial book progress still persists). Full cinema/music completion trains once after the successful domain history mutation and session consumption. Partial or cancelled attendance does not train. Completed social sessions retain the existing full-member-only training rule, now through the common recorder. Drinks train the generic GET_COFFEE habit only on the first successful sip of a new serving, in addition to the already-existing beverage-specific preference count.
Common recorder preserves existing habit map, cap 0.30 and increment 0.012; adds cumulative success count, last simulation time, room, station, subject/context, four six-hour time buckets and bounded recent 32 actions. Effective learned strength decays on access with a provisional 14 simulated-day half-life; old unproven habits retained without fabricated history or timestamp. No offline decay or change to production duration constants.
